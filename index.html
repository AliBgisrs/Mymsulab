Research program

Methods that scale, from plots to regions

The lab works where remote sensing, GeoAI and agronomy meet. We validate on research plots and deploy across producer fields and whole regions.

01

Irrigation mapping with deep learning

YOLOv11 · 0.98 precision · 0.94 F1

Automated detection of center-pivot irrigation systems from Landsat 8, Sentinel-2 and NAIP imagery. A custom ArcGIS tool handles data prep and large-scale inference. YOLOv11 outperformed U-Net, Faster R-CNN and Mask R-CNN, a step toward sustainable groundwater management in semi-arid regions.

  • YOLOv11
  • Sentinel-2
  • ArcGIS
  • Water management
02

UAS phenotyping of field crops

YOLOv12 stand counts · R² 0.972

Multispectral data integration and stacked ensemble learning to estimate yield, protein and maturity in dry peas; YOLOv12 segmentation for dry bean stand counting; and a synthesis of 211 studies on measuring above-ground biomass with UAS sensors, from RGB to hyperspectral and LiDAR.

  • Multispectral
  • Ensemble learning
  • Dry pea · dry bean
  • Biomass
03

Hyperspectral imaging & seed quality

Classifying seeds and assessing quality traits from hyperspectral imagery, bringing lab-grade spectroscopy into high-throughput pipelines for breeding programs and seed-industry partners.

  • Hyperspectral
  • Classification
  • Seed quality
04

Precision agriculture for Montana

A producer-engaged program at MSU: decision-support tools, variable-rate strategies and on-farm experimentation tuned to the dryland and irrigated systems of the Northern Great Plains.

  • Decision support
  • On-farm trials
  • Variable rate
  • Montana
